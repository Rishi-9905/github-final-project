# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting different opinions.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, and disrespectful comments.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.
